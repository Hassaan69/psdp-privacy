Government of Balochistan

Official Privacy Policy for the Public Sector Development Programme (PSDP) progress tracking and schemes monitoring application.

Effective Date: July 1, 2026 Last Updated: July 1, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how the Planning & Development (P&D) Department, Government of Balochistan ("we", "us", or "our") handles, stores, and protects user data and device information when using the PSDP Schemes Monitoring App ("the App"). The App is specifically designed for official government monitors, engineers, and authorized personnel to document, evaluate, and track the progress of development schemes across Balochistan.

Data Collection & Processing

The App is designed with an offline-first architecture. It collects progress data, geolocations, and site photographs from users. All gathered data is stored securely in a local database (WatermelonDB) on your device. Once an internet connection is established, the data is synced and uploaded directly to the secure servers of the Planning & Development Department. We do not transmit or share your data with any third-party services or private entities.

Device Permissions Required

To perform its progress-monitoring operations successfully, the App requests access to the following device hardware and system features:

Camera Mandatory

Allows the App to capture high-quality site photographs. These photos are used to document the physical progress of schemes and verify completed construction works.

Location Services Mandatory

Allows the App to capture precise GPS coordinates. These coordinates are strictly used to geo-tag site progress reports and verify that the monitoring activity was conducted on-site at the exact scheme location.

Microphone Optional

Allows the App to record audio notes or attachments during site visits, if required by the monitoring officer to provide supplementary details.

Data Security

We are committed to securing your data. All communication between the App and the department's backend servers is encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer (SSL/TLS). Local data stored on the device via WatermelonDB is secured within the App's sandbox, preventing other applications from accessing it.

User Access Control

Access to the App is strictly limited to registered government employees, monitors, and contractors approved by the Planning & Development Department. Registration, authorization, and authentication are managed centrally through official department portals.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in application functionality or regulatory guidelines. We will notify users of any substantial changes by posting the updated policy on this page or inside the App.
